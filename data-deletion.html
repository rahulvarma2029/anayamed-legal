Your right to erasure

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask us to delete the personal data we hold about you. This page explains how to ask, what happens, and the narrow set of records we are legally required to keep.

How to request deletion

  1. Email privacy@anayamed.com from the email address registered on your AnayaMED account, with the subject line Data Deletion Request.
  2. Tell us which account. Include the mobile number registered with AnayaMED. Please do not send us your password, an OTP, or any clinical information.
  3. Verify it is you. We will send a one-time passcode to the registered mobile number or email. This step protects you — without it, anyone who knew your number could erase your medical communications.
  4. We confirm. You will receive written confirmation of what was deleted and what was retained, with the reason for any retention.

If you cannot access your registered email or number, contact us anyway and we will agree an alternative way to verify your identity.

How long it takes

StageTiming
We acknowledge your requestWithin 3 working days
Identity verified and deletion carried outWithin 30 days of verification
Removal from backupsWithin a further 90 days, as backups rotate

We will tell you if a request will take longer, and why.

What gets deleted

  • Your mobile number and email address
  • Your name and preferred name
  • Your language and notification preferences
  • Your consent records
  • Any live one-time passcodes and cached data (these expire within minutes in any case)
  • The content of notifications we sent you

Once deleted, we can no longer contact you through AnayaMED, and your account will no longer function.

What we must keep, and why

We cannot delete everything. The DPDP Act permits a Data Fiduciary to retain personal data where another law requires it. In our case:

This is the evidence that you were told about an appointment, a result or a prescription. It does not contain the message content.

Clinical records created during a consultationAs required by medical record-keeping lawHeld separately from the communications data described here, and governed by their own retention rules.

Retained records are kept only for these purposes. They are not used to contact you, and not used for any commercial purpose.

Deleting data held by WhatsApp

Messages we sent you over WhatsApp are also present on your own device and in Meta's systems. Deleting your AnayaMED data does not remove them from WhatsApp. To remove those, delete the conversation in the WhatsApp app, and see WhatsApp's own help pages for their data practices.

A narrower option: stop the messages, keep the account

If you want the messages to stop but do not want your account erased, you do not need a deletion request. In the AnayaMED app, open Settings → Notifications and switch off any category. Sign-in passcodes and safety-critical alerts continue, because they are how we verify it is you and how we reach you in an emergency.

If you are not satisfied

Contact our Grievance Officer. If we do not resolve your complaint, you may escalate to the Data Protection Board of India.
