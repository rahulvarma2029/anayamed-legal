1. Who we are

AnayaMed Healthtech Private Limited ("AnayaMED", "we") operates a healthcare platform connecting patients with clinicians. For the personal data described in this policy we act as a Data Fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act").

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers the personal data we process in order to send you messages — appointment confirmations and cancellations, one-time passcodes (OTPs) for signing in, and other notifications about your care — over WhatsApp, SMS and email.

It does not cover clinical records created during a consultation, which are governed separately. Where the two overlap, the stricter protection applies.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use your contact details for advertising, and we do not share them with advertisers.
  • We do not send marketing messages on the basis of your having booked an appointment.

4. Our lawful basis

We process this data on the basis of your consent, and for the legitimate uses permitted under the DPDP Act where you have approached us for a service.

Notifications are grouped, and you control them by group:

GroupExamplesDefault
EssentialSign-in OTPs, safety-critical alertsAlways sent — these cannot be switched off while you hold an account, because they are how we verify it is you and how we reach you in an emergency
ServiceAppointment reminders, prescriptions, lab reports, billing, health recordsOn — you may switch any of them off
OptionalMedication reminders, care programmes, health tipsOff — sent only if you switch them on

We do not send Service or Optional messages between 22:00 and 07:00 IST. Messages that would fall in that window are held and sent the following morning.

SMS in India is additionally governed by TRAI's Distributed Ledger Technology framework. The message templates we send are registered with, and visible to, the telecom operators who carry them.

6. Where your data is processed

Our own systems — databases, application servers and backups — run in India (Google Cloud, Mumbai region). MSG91 processes SMS in India.

WhatsApp messages are an exception you should know about. Meta's WhatsApp Business Cloud API routes messages through Meta's global infrastructure, which means your mobile number and the content of a WhatsApp message may be processed outside India. If you would prefer not to have your data leave India, switch WhatsApp off in your notification settings and we will use SMS or email instead.

7. How we protect it

  • Mobile numbers are stored only as irreversible SHA-256 hashes in our notification databases and caches — never in plain text. Your number is fetched at the moment a message is sent and is not retained afterwards.
  • One-time passcodes are stored only as hashes, expire after 10 minutes, and are invalidated after three incorrect attempts.
  • Message content is not written to our application logs. Logs record which type of message was sent, never the message itself, your number, or an OTP.
  • Access to production systems is restricted and audited.

9. Your rights under the DPDP Act

  • Access — a summary of the personal data we process about you and who we have shared it with.
  • Correction — to correct data that is inaccurate, and complete data that is incomplete.
  • Erasure — to have your data deleted. See Data Deletion Instructions for how, and for the limited records we must keep by law.
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, as easily as you gave it. Withdrawal does not affect anything done before you withdrew.
  • Grievance redressal — to complain to us first, and then to the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied.
  • Nominate — to name someone to exercise these rights if you die or become incapacitated.

We respond within 30 days.

10. Children

Where we know a patient is under 18, we process their data only with verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian, and we do not send optional or promotional messages to them.
